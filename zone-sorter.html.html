Awareness Zone Sorter

A sample participant, invented for practice. For each behavior, choose the zone it lands in.

Two questions decide the zone. Is The Full Picture 5.0 or higher? Is My Own View 1.25 or more away from The Full Picture?
ZoneWhat places a behavior here
Recognized StrengthThe Full Picture is 5.0 or higher, and My Own View is within 1.25 of it.
Underestimated StrengthThe Full Picture is 5.0 or higher, and My Own View is 1.25 or more below it.
Overestimated BehaviorMy Own View is 1.25 or more above The Full Picture, whether The Full Picture is high or low.
